Summer Internship-I

Source: Revised 2022 syllabus, p.22. 1 credit.

What it is

Inter/intra-institutional training, simulation programmes in institutes/technical colleges, or another department of the same polytechnic.

When

4 weeks after the 2nd semester.

What a student keeps

The syllabus does not prescribe an evidence list. The items below are a suggested personal checklist, not an official requirement — ask your institution for the actual submission rules.

  • Dated work notes or a logbook of what you did each day.
  • Any training confirmation, offer letter or attendance record the host gave you.
  • Any assessment, certificate or completion document the institution or host issued.
  • Your own report draft, kept privately; do not upload another person's data.

No internship report, template or graded work is provided here.
