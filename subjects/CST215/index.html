CST215 — Data Structures Lab

Practical course. Source: Revised 2022 syllabus, p.19. Listed areas are transcribed tasks, not completed solutions.

  • Array operations: insertion, deletion
  • Stacks: push/pop, infix to postfix, postfix evaluation
  • Recursive programs: factorial, Fibonacci, Ackermann, Tower of Hanoi
  • Queue operations; recursion converted to iteration
  • Linked lists: singly, doubly, circular; merge and reverse
  • Trees: creation, traversals, deletion, binary search tree
  • Sorting techniques (minimum three)
  • Searching techniques (minimum two)
  • Graph theory assignments
