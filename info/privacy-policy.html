Quiz storage and reset

Quiz attempts are local snapshots under diploma-3rd-sem-attempt-*; legacy quiz-autosave-* saves are retained until verified migration. quiz-history, quiz-result, and quiz-pending-config store history, results and settings. diploma-3rd-sem-quiz-mistakes stores the mistake queue and resolution count. No quiz data is uploaded. Clear history, discard attempt and clear mistake queue are separate controls; homepage reset does not clear them all. Clear browser site data to remove all local quiz data.

Current storage boundary and backup

Student records now pass through the first-party storage module and use the d3s: namespace. localStorage remains the primary store; a same-origin IndexedDB mirror (d3s-store) keeps a recovery copy of each record so an evicted or cleared origin can be rebuilt, and clearing everything deletes the mirror too. The mirror is never the only copy of a record and never leaves this device. Legacy records migrate on read after a verified copy. Old quiz/theme key names above describe compatibility names, not separate current stores.

Settings and backup shows record counts and estimated bytes, offers independent group resets, and requires typing RESET for everything. The homepage's older reset is not a substitute. Backups contain private local data and are downloaded or copied only at your request. No backup is uploaded. Interrupted imports temporarily retain original records in d3s:restore-journal for rollback; exports during pending recovery return those originals. A session-only usefulness marker prevents repeated prompts in the same tab and is not part of student-data backups. Origin storage estimates also include resource caches.

Resource requests fetch first-party pages, scripts, styles, icons and derived data. Service workers cache visited resources. Optional email feedback opens your email client; it is separate from the local storage system.
