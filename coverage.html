Syllabus coverage and verification

Syllabus reference: Revised 2022 syllabus. The syllabus declares 5 theory courses, 3 laboratories and 1 internship, with 23 theory units. The site ships 5 theory overview pages, 23 syllabus unit pages, 3 practical pages and 1 internship page.

Theory counts use stored tags, not verified topic mappings. Practical pages list the syllabus tasks only: no completed lab solution is claimed. Counts include archived duplicates, so a number is an inventory, not a coverage score.
